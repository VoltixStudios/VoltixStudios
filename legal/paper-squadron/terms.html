Your licence to play

Downloading Paper Squadron gives you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to play it on devices you control, for as long as it is available. The game, its planes, its artwork and its name remain the property of Voltix Studios. You may record and stream your own play, including monetised video. You may not resell the game, redistribute its files, or modify it to change what a purchase or an advertisement grants, or to change a score.

Folds and STARS

Folds and STARS are virtual items, not money. They have no value outside the game, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be transferred to another player.

  • Folds are earned in every flight and can also be bought in packs. They buy planes, weapons, wingmen and upgrades in the hangar.
  • STARS are dropped by every boss and can also be bought in packs. Together with folds they buy the Frost Peaks and Desert Kingdom kit and the last upgrade levels.

Buying folds or STARS buys a limited licence to use a feature of the game, not an asset you own. Unspent folds and STARS are not property, cannot be redeemed for money, and are lost with the save that holds them. Nothing in this paragraph limits a statutory right you have as a consumer.

What is on sale

  • Remove Ads: removes the advertisement shown between runs, for good. The optional rewarded advertisements stay available, by your choice.
  • Starter bundle: folds and STARS, once.
  • Folds, in three pack sizes.
  • STARS, in two pack sizes.

What each item gives is stated on Google Play's purchase sheet before you pay. Every price you are shown comes from Google Play, in your own currency, including any tax that applies where you live. No price appears in this document, because the one in the store is the one that governs. Nothing is a subscription, and nothing sold for money is a randomised purchase: you always know exactly what you get before you pay.

Advertisements

A rewarded advertisement is always your choice: the button says what it gives first (a revive, double folds, or the shop's sale), and declining costs nothing. A video closed before its end gives no reward. An advertisement may also be shown between runs; Remove Ads ends that one. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent before any advertisement loads, and you can change your answer at any time with the Ad Privacy button on the game's main screen.

Callsigns, the leaderboard and Play Games

If you submit a score to the weekly survival leaderboard, your callsign and score are shown to other players. Choose a callsign that is not offensive, does not pretend to be someone else and does not contain anyone's personal details. We may remove a callsign or a score that breaks this, or a score obtained by modifying the game, without notice. You can delete your callsign, scores and cloud save at any time (Delete your data).

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. Achievements and Play Games leaderboards belong to your Google account and are governed by Google's own terms; we cannot change or delete them.

What money never buys

  • A world, a plane, a weapon or a wingman. Every one of them can be earned in play: folds come from every flight and STARS from every boss.
  • A place on the leaderboard by any means other than flying for it.

Refunds and withdrawal

Purchases are made through Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Because what you buy is delivered to the game at once, you agree, when you buy, that it is supplied immediately, which under EU consumer law means the 14-day right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins. If something you paid for did not arrive, write to us and we will put it right.

Losing your progress

Your progress lives on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, it is also kept in your cloud save, which brings it back on another phone; without signing in, we cannot restore a save that was deleted. Remove Ads is restored from Google Play when you reinstall; folds and STARS already delivered live in your save.

Changes to the game

We improve the game over time: planes, worlds, prices of items bought with folds or STARS and what a pack holds may change. We will not take away something you bought with money: when paper and clips left the game in version 1.8.0, every one a player held became folds. If the game is ever withdrawn, we will say so on this site in advance.

Liability and governing law

The game is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, Voltix Studios is not liable for indirect loss arising from playing it. Nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer under the law of the country where you live. These terms are governed by the law of Spain.

Un anuncio con recompensa siempre es decisión tuya: el botón dice primero lo que da (revivir, folds dobles o la oferta de la tienda), y decir que no no cuesta nada. Un vídeo cerrado antes de terminar no da recompensa. También puede aparecer un anuncio entre partidas; Quitar anuncios lo elimina. En el Espacio Económico Europeo y el Reino Unido se te pide el consentimiento antes de cargar ningún anuncio, y puedes cambiar tu respuesta cuando quieras con el botón Privacidad de anuncios de la pantalla principal del juego.

Indicativos, la clasificación y Play Juegos

Si envías una puntuación a la clasificación semanal de supervivencia, tu indicativo y tu puntuación se muestran a otros jugadores. Elige un indicativo que no sea ofensivo, que no se haga pasar por otra persona y que no contenga datos personales de nadie. Podemos retirar sin aviso un indicativo o una puntuación que incumpla esto, o una puntuación obtenida modificando el juego. Puedes borrar tu indicativo, tus puntuaciones y tu partida en la nube cuando quieras (Eliminar tus datos).

Iniciar sesión con Google Play Juegos es opcional. Los logros y las clasificaciones de Play Juegos pertenecen a tu cuenta de Google y se rigen por las condiciones de Google; no podemos cambiarlos ni borrarlos.

Lo que el dinero nunca compra

  • Un mundo, un avión, un arma o un escolta. Todos se pueden ganar jugando: los folds salen de cada vuelo y las STARS de cada jefe.
  • Un puesto en la clasificación por otro camino que no sea volar.

Reembolsos y desistimiento

Las compras se hacen en Google Play, y los reembolsos siguen la política de reembolsos de Google Play. Como lo que compras se entrega en el juego al momento, al comprar aceptas que se te suministre de inmediato, lo que según la normativa europea de consumo significa que el derecho de desistimiento de 14 días termina en cuanto empieza la entrega. Si algo que pagaste no llegó, escríbenos y lo arreglamos.

Perder tu progreso

Tu progreso vive en tu teléfono. Si inicias sesión con Google Play Juegos, también se guarda en tu partida en la nube, que lo recupera en otro teléfono; sin iniciar sesión, no podemos recuperar una partida borrada. Quitar anuncios se restaura desde Google Play al reinstalar; los folds y las STARS ya entregados viven en tu partida.

Cambios en el juego

Mejoramos el juego con el tiempo: los aviones, los mundos, los precios de lo que se compra con folds o STARS y lo que contiene un paquete pueden cambiar. No te quitaremos nada que hayas comprado con dinero: cuando el papel y los clips salieron del juego en la versión 1.8.0, cada uno que tenía un jugador se convirtió en folds. Si alguna vez retiramos el juego, lo anunciaremos antes en esta web.
