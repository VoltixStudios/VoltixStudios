How to play

  1. One cloth a day, the same one for every player in the world.
  2. The starting cloth has to become the pattern. Both are at the top of the screen.
  3. Every operation you need is already in the tray. What is missing is their order.
  4. Tap them into the slots, then press Weave. They run on the cloth one at a time.
  5. Then you are told how many are in place, and the slots are ticked from the first one for as long as the order is right. Change the rest and weave again.
Tap the mark on an operation to watch what it does to a cloth. It never costs an attempt.
