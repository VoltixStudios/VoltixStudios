Paper Squadron

Capture. Defend. Dominate.

Fold a paper plane, launch it off the desk, and fly.

An arcade flight game about a squadron of paper aircraft fighting across a world made of stationery — a classroom of notebook mountains, a pirate bay of cardboard ships, a frozen range of paper peaks, and now a desert kingdom where sand tornadoes stand over the dunes.

Tilt your phone to fly, or switch to touch steering if you prefer. Press and hold the right side of the screen to fire. New to flying? Flight School teaches the basics in five short missions.

Get it on Google Play

New world

Desert Kingdom

Sand tornadoes that stand most of the way to the flight ceiling, scarab swarms, and raiders that burrow under the dunes. At the end of it waits THE SAND KING, a paper cobra that rears out of the sand with its hood spread.

Clear it for the Dune Falcon, an engineless dart whose DASH trades the boost for a shielded burst straight through the enemy, the Boomerang Cards that cut harder on the way home, and the Desert Hawk.

  • Four worlds

    Classroom Skies, Pirate Bay, Frost Peaks and Desert Kingdom, each with its own enemies, hazards, music and boss — and every run deals its missions in a new order.

  • Ten kinds of mission

    Clear the skies, hold out against waves, chase down an enemy ace, destroy ground bases, collect cargo, fly through ring gates, beat elite waves, or fly multi-stage operations back to back.

  • Bosses with teeth

    Every world ends in a multi-phase fight. Learn its patterns, break its armour, and bring it down.

  • Build your squadron

    Clear a world to unlock its plane, weapon and wingman. Spend folds on hull, speed, agility, luck, damage and fire rate, and fly with two wingmen at once.

  • Weekly survival

    An endless, steadily harder run in a world that rotates every week, with global ranks — and a supply drop waiting for everyone who flew it.

  • Save and come back

    Save & Exit from the pause menu, and RESUME later picks up the same mission where the run left off.

The worlds

  • Classroom Skies

    Notebook mountains and pencil towers. Clear it for the Falcon Mk-I.

  • Pirate Bay

    Cardboard ships, cannon pods and treasure. Clear it for the Corsair Raider.

  • Frost Peaks

    Blizzards, icicles and frozen fortresses. Clear it for the F-17 Frost Hawk.

  • Desert Kingdom New

    Sand tornadoes, scarab swarms and THE SAND KING. Clear it for the Dune Falcon.

The hangar

Every craft is folded around a real fuselage now: wings creased along their length, winglets, intakes and fins, and a livery drawn in marker ink.
