Your licence to play

Voltix Studios grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to install and play CoreWard on devices you control, for your own non-commercial use. The game, its artwork, its name and its code remain the property of Voltix Studios. Your use of the app is also subject to the Google Play Terms of Service.

Cores

Cores are a virtual item with no real-world value. They are non-refundable and non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for money or goods outside the game.

Cores are earned by playing and can also be bought. Buying them grants you a limited licence to use them inside CoreWard; it does not give you ownership of anything, and no balance of Cores is property, credit, a deposit or a payment instrument. They cannot be sold, gifted, traded, transferred between accounts or devices, or converted back into money.

The real-money price is shown next to every Cores amount on sale, in your own currency, before you confirm anything. Cores are spent inside the game on boosts, permanent upgrades, drone chassis, spare hulls and continues. Every one of those is also reachable by playing — money changes how long something takes, never whether it is possible.

Cores do not expire while the game is available. If CoreWard is discontinued, see Changes to the game.

Artifacts are never sold

Artifacts are the game's second resource. They are found in the shaft and nothing else: no purchase grants one, no amount of money produces one, and they carry no price of any kind. They are mentioned here because they appear next to Cores in the game's store screen, and a second resource shown without a price could otherwise be mistaken for a currency whose cost had been left off. It has not — artifacts are simply not for sale.

What is on sale

Five Cores packs, a one-time starter kit, and two one-time purchases that affect ads:

  • Remove ads — removes the full-screen ad break. The optional rewarded videos remain available, because they are how a free player earns Cores and removing them would reduce your income rather than improve your experience.
  • Supporter pack — removes the ad break, pays out the rewarded placements without playing a video at all, adds a permanent increase to the Cores you earn, and includes a cosmetic skin.

Both are one purchase per account and are restored through Google Play if you reinstall or change device — use Store → Restore purchases in the game. Prices and contents are shown in the store screen before you confirm, and those shown in the app at the time of purchase are the ones that apply.

No loot boxes, no randomised rewards

Nothing sold in CoreWard is randomised. There are no chests, no odds, no surprise contents and no mechanic where money buys a chance at something. You always know exactly what a purchase contains before you make it.

Refunds and withdrawal

All purchases are processed by Google Play, and refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy. Contact us at the address below if something went wrong with a purchase and we will help where we can.

If you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you normally have a 14-day right to withdraw from a digital purchase. That right ends once delivery of the digital content has begun with your consent — and because Cores and the ad-removal entitlements are delivered to your game immediately on purchase, that is generally at once. Nothing here limits any consumer right you have that cannot be waived by agreement.

Losing your progress

CoreWard has no account and no cloud save: everything, including Cores you have bought, is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or moving to a new device will lose your progress and any unspent Cores with it. The two ad-removal purchases are the exception — they are tied to your Google account and can be restored. Please keep this in mind before uninstalling.

We may update CoreWard, adjust game balance, and add, change or retire content. If the game is discontinued, we will make reasonable efforts to give notice through the store listing beforehand; unspent Cores cannot be refunded or exchanged at that point, which is part of why they are described as they are above. If these terms change, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

Núcleos

Los núcleos son un artículo virtual sin valor en el mundo real. No son reembolsables ni transferibles, y no pueden canjearse por dinero ni bienes fuera del juego.

Los núcleos se obtienen jugando y también pueden comprarse. Comprarlos te concede una licencia limitada para usarlos dentro de CoreWard; no te da la propiedad de nada, y ningún saldo de núcleos constituye patrimonio, crédito, depósito ni instrumento de pago. No pueden venderse, regalarse, intercambiarse, transferirse entre cuentas o dispositivos, ni convertirse de nuevo en dinero.

El precio en dinero real se muestra junto a cada cantidad de núcleos a la venta, en tu propia moneda, antes de que confirmes nada. Los núcleos se gastan dentro del juego en impulsos, mejoras permanentes, chasis de dron, cascos de repuesto y continuaciones. Todo ello es alcanzable también jugando: el dinero cambia cuánto se tarda en algo, nunca si es posible.

Los núcleos no caducan mientras el juego esté disponible. Si CoreWard se descontinúa, consulta Cambios en el juego.

Los artefactos nunca se venden

Los artefactos son el segundo recurso del juego. Se encuentran en el pozo y en ningún otro sitio: ninguna compra otorga uno, ninguna cantidad de dinero produce uno y no tienen precio de ningún tipo. Se mencionan aquí porque aparecen junto a los núcleos en la pantalla de tienda del juego, y un segundo recurso mostrado sin precio podría confundirse con una moneda a la que se le hubiera omitido el coste. No es así: los artefactos sencillamente no están a la venta.

Qué está a la venta

Cinco paquetes de núcleos, un kit inicial de compra única y dos compras únicas que afectan a los anuncios:

  • Quitar anuncios — elimina la pausa publicitaria a pantalla completa. Los vídeos recompensados opcionales siguen disponibles, porque son la forma en que un jugador gratuito gana núcleos y quitarlos reduciría tus ingresos en lugar de mejorar tu experiencia.
  • Pack de mecenas — elimina la pausa publicitaria, paga las recompensas sin necesidad de reproducir ningún vídeo, añade un aumento permanente de los núcleos que ganas e incluye un aspecto cosmético.

Ambas son una compra por cuenta y se restauran a través de Google Play si reinstalas o cambias de dispositivo: usa Tienda → Restaurar compras en el juego. Los precios y contenidos se muestran en la pantalla de tienda antes de confirmar, y los que aparezcan en la aplicación en el momento de la compra son los aplicables.

Nada de lo que se vende en CoreWard es aleatorio. No hay cofres, ni probabilidades, ni contenidos sorpresa, ni ninguna mecánica en la que el dinero compre una posibilidad de obtener algo. Siempre sabes exactamente qué contiene una compra antes de hacerla.

Todas las compras las procesa Google Play, y los reembolsos se gestionan según la política de reembolsos de Google Play. Escríbenos a la dirección de abajo si algo ha salido mal con una compra y te ayudaremos en lo que podamos.

Perder tu progreso

CoreWard no tiene cuenta ni copia en la nube: todo, incluidos los núcleos que hayas comprado, se guarda en tu dispositivo. Desinstalar el juego, borrar sus datos o pasar a un dispositivo nuevo hará que pierdas tu progreso y con él los núcleos sin gastar. Las dos compras que eliminan anuncios son la excepción: están vinculadas a tu cuenta de Google y pueden restaurarse. Tenlo presente antes de desinstalar.
