Your licence to play

Downloading Pocket Foundry gives you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to play it on devices you control, for as long as it is available. The game, its puzzles, its artwork and its name remain the property of Voltix Studios. You may record and stream your own play, including monetised video. You may not resell the game, redistribute its files, or modify it to change what a purchase or an advertisement grants.

Cogs, crystals and hints

Crystals are a virtual item, not money. They have no value outside the game, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be transferred to another player.

  • Cogs are earned by filling orders and from your workshops. They are never sold for money.
  • Hints are earned in play, or taken for cogs, crystals or a rewarded advertisement. They are never sold for money on their own.
  • Crystals are earned in play, from contracts, the season track and perfect islands, and can also be bought in packs. They buy special decorations, hints, the full solution of an order you are stuck on, and a new contract.

Buying crystals buys a limited licence to use a feature of the game, not an asset you own. Unspent crystals are not property, cannot be redeemed for money, and are lost with the save that holds them. Nothing in this paragraph limits a statutory right you have as a consumer.

What is on sale

  • Starter crate: no ad breaks, crystals and a special decoration. Offered once, after your second repair, and never at first launch. Kept for good.
  • No ads: no ad breaks between orders, for good. Rewarded advertisements stay available, by your choice.
  • The Foundry Pass for the current season, described below.
  • Crystals, in three pack sizes.

Every price you are shown comes from Google Play, in your own currency, including any tax that applies where you live. No price appears in this document, because the one in the store is the one that governs. Nothing is a subscription, and nothing is a randomised purchase: you always know exactly what you get before you pay.

The Foundry Pass and the season

A season lasts about four weeks. Stars come only from what can be done every day: the Daily Order, contracts, new production-line orders and the daily reward. Every tier of stars opens a free reward for everyone. The Foundry Pass is a one-time purchase for one season that opens a second reward on each tier. It does not give stars, open tiers sooner or skip anything. Rewards you claimed are yours to keep after the season ends; rewards not claimed by the end of the season are not carried into the next one. Each season's Pass is a separate purchase.

Advertisements

A rewarded advertisement is always your choice: the button says what it gives first, and you can decline. A short ad break may also appear between orders, never in your first six orders, and never for anyone who owns No ads or the Starter crate. A video closed before its end gives no reward.

What money never buys

  • A solved puzzle with full marks. A bought solution caps that order's marks.
  • A new island, an order or a rule. Every puzzle in the game can be reached and solved without paying.

Refunds and withdrawal

Purchases are made through Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Because what you buy is delivered to the game at once, you agree, when you buy, that it is supplied immediately, which under EU consumer law means the 14-day right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins. If something you paid for did not arrive, write to us and we will put it right.

Losing your progress

Your progress lives on your phone and in Android's backup of it. There is no account, so we cannot restore a save that was deleted on the phone and in the backup. Bought products that are kept for good (the Starter crate, No ads and a season's Pass) are restored from Google Play when you reinstall; crystals already delivered live in the save.

Changes to the game

We improve the game over time: puzzles, prices of items bought with cogs or crystals, and the season track may change. We will not take away something you bought with money. If the game is ever withdrawn, we will say so on this site in advance.

Liability and governing law

The game is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, Voltix Studios is not liable for indirect loss arising from playing it. Nothing in these terms limits your rights as a consumer under the law of the country where you live. These terms are governed by the law of Spain.

Engranajes, cristales y pistas

Los cristales son un objeto virtual, no dinero. No tienen valor fuera del juego, no se pueden cambiar por dinero y no se pueden transferir a otro jugador.

  • Los engranajes se ganan completando pedidos y en tus talleres. Nunca se venden por dinero.
  • Las pistas se ganan jugando, o se obtienen con engranajes, cristales o un anuncio con recompensa. Nunca se venden por dinero por separado.
  • Los cristales se ganan jugando, con contratos, la ruta de la temporada y las islas perfectas, y también se pueden comprar en paquetes. Sirven para decoraciones especiales, pistas, la solución completa de un pedido en el que te has atascado y un contrato nuevo.

Comprar cristales es comprar una licencia limitada para usar una función del juego, no un bien de tu propiedad. Los cristales sin gastar no son propiedad, no se pueden canjear por dinero y se pierden con la partida que los contiene. Nada de este párrafo limita un derecho que tengas por ley como consumidor.

  • Sin anuncios: sin pausas publicitarias entre pedidos, para siempre. Los anuncios con recompensa siguen disponibles, si tú quieres.
  • El Pase de la Fundición de la temporada en curso, descrito abajo.
  • Cristales, en paquetes de tres tamaños.

Todos los precios que ves vienen de Google Play, en tu moneda e incluyendo los impuestos que se apliquen donde vives. Este documento no incluye precios, porque el que manda es el de la tienda. Nada es una suscripción y nada es una compra aleatoria: siempre sabes exactamente lo que recibes antes de pagar.

El Pase de la Fundición y la temporada

Una temporada dura unas cuatro semanas. Las estrellas solo se ganan con lo que se puede hacer cada día: el Pedido del día, los contratos, los pedidos nuevos de las líneas de producción y la recompensa diaria. Cada nivel de estrellas abre una recompensa gratuita para todos. El Pase de la Fundición es una compra única para una temporada que abre una segunda recompensa en cada nivel. No da estrellas, no abre niveles antes ni salta nada. Las recompensas que reclames son tuyas cuando acaba la temporada; las que no reclames antes del final no pasan a la siguiente. El Pase de cada temporada es una compra aparte.

Anuncios

Un anuncio con recompensa siempre es decisión tuya: el botón dice primero lo que da, y puedes decir que no. También puede aparecer una pausa publicitaria breve entre pedidos, nunca en tus seis primeros pedidos y nunca a quien tenga Sin anuncios o la Caja de inicio. Un vídeo cerrado antes de terminar no da recompensa.

Lo que el dinero nunca compra

  • Un puzle resuelto con todas las marcas. Una solución comprada limita las marcas de ese pedido.
  • Una isla, un pedido o una regla nuevos. Todos los puzles del juego se pueden alcanzar y resolver sin pagar.

Reembolsos y desistimiento

Las compras se hacen en Google Play, y los reembolsos siguen la política de reembolsos de Google Play. Como lo que compras se entrega en el juego al momento, al comprar aceptas que se te suministre de inmediato, lo que según la normativa europea de consumo significa que el derecho de desistimiento de 14 días termina en cuanto empieza la entrega. Si algo que pagaste no llegó, escríbenos y lo arreglamos.

Perder tu progreso

Tu progreso vive en tu teléfono y en la copia de seguridad que hace Android. No hay cuenta, así que no podemos recuperar una partida borrada del teléfono y de la copia. Los productos comprados que son para siempre (la Caja de inicio, Sin anuncios y el Pase de una temporada) se restauran desde Google Play al reinstalar; los cristales ya entregados viven en la partida.
