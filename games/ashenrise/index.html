AshenRise

Heroes of Light.

A whole world has fallen into shadow. You are light.

Fight through three warrior lands swallowed by darkness: the Land of the Rising Sun, the Frozen Fjords and the City of Bronze. Thirty levels, six bosses, and a fight you feel in your hands.

A white glint is a blow you can block: block at the last moment and counter. A red glow cannot be blocked: dodge or jump. Fill your energy, unleash your weapon's special, and the fallen rise again as warriors of light to fight at your side.

Offline, with no energy to wait for. Play as long as you like.

  • A real fight

    Three-hit combos, air combos, a double jump, lunges and plunges. Criticals and heavy blows cut enemies in two.

  • Arise!

    The ember storm, the eternal winter, the thunder judgement, the abyss, the stone gaze. After the special, the fallen join you, and every boss you beat joins your army as a commander.

  • Three heroes

    The Dawn Warden, balanced, with a sun-blade. The Moon Dancer, fast and deadly, with twin crescent blades. The Storm Titan, tough and brutal, with a thunder hammer.

  • Gear that shows

    Weapons, helmets, armour and rings that show on your hero, every piece with its own look. Every boss drops its own pieces.

  • Powers to build on

    Life steal, chain lightning, burning and turning foes to stone. Fuse, enhance and star up what you find.

  • Fair odds

    The odds of every random item are shown before you buy. Blood can be turned off in Settings.
