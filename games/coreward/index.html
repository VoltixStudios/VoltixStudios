CoreWard

Descend. Survive. Restore.

Drill down. Read the collapse before it happens.

The surface has cooled past habitability. The last settlement survives on geothermal tap lines, and those lines are failing. You pilot single-use survey drones down through the crust, mapping strata and reaching viable heat pockets before the drone cooks.

A vertical-descent arcade puzzler in portrait, played with one thumb, in runs of two to five minutes.

Get it on Google Play

  • Drilling

    Move into a block to drill it. Down is fast, sideways slower, up slowest and rarely correct.

  • Gravity

    Unsupported blocks fall after a beat, and the beat shortens with depth. Being underneath one when it lands ends the run — reading collapses before they happen is the whole skill.

  • Seams

    Drilling takes the entire connected body of that mineral, not just the cell under the bit. The hole is as large as the body you chose to bite into.

  • Fusion

    Four or more of the same mineral in contact fuse and vanish — which is not always what you want. Colour matching becomes a routing decision.

  • Heat

    The gauge is the clock. It climbs faster the deeper you go, and coolant caches are scattered thin and never in the middle column.

  • Sixty tiers

    Twelve to a stratum, with objectives that change on the way down: reach the floor, reach it without a cinnabar fusion, reach it with the drone already hot. The hard tiers show on the map while they are still locked.

  • Three ways down

    Expedition is the sixty tiers at your own pace. Deep Dive is endless from the surface with no boosts. Daily Shaft is one shaft from today's date, the same rock for everyone, and one attempt.

  • Shape markers

    A switch in Settings gives every mineral its own silhouette as well as its own colour, for anyone who would rather not tell cinnabar from pyrite by hue alone.

  • The rules stay honest

    Nothing in the economy can extend the fall delay. Reading a collapse is the skill, and it is not for sale.

Five minerals, five outcomes

MineralWhat it does
Basalt Inert. A fusion vanishes cleanly — neutral filler.
Cinnabar Runs hot. A fusion releases heat and the gauge jumps — route around it.
Cryolite Coolant. A fusion vents it and the gauge drops — hunt for it.
Pyrite Worth Cores. A fusion pays a score multiplier — the greed play.
Nitre A charge, not a rock. Drill it and a moment later the whole body goes off, blasting a cross out of the shaft. Pick the moment, then get clear. Found only below the sediment.

Also in the rock

  • Reinforced rock

    Five hits to cut, and it never fuses. An obstacle, not a mineral.

  • Steel ledge

    From tier 19, a shelf of plate in the drone's line. Eight hot hits to cut, so going round is quicker. It never falls: in a collapse, the ground under it is the safe place to stand.

  • Coolant cache

    Drill it and the gauge comes down, no match needed. Scattered thin, and never in the middle column.

  • Artifact

    At most one a tier, in the lower half and off the straight line. It pays in Cores, and it is never for sale.
